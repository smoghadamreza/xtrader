# Data

The **Data** app is a comprehensive Django application designed to manage and analyze financial and stock
market information. It provides models for storing key financial data, including ratios (Ratio), 
income statements ([](income-model.md)), and balance sheets ([](balance-sheet-model.md)), allowing users to track
and evaluate company performance. Additionally, the app includes models for monitoring 
stock market activity ([](stock-watch-model.md)) and broader financial market trends ([](market-watch-model.md)), 
offering tools for detailed financial analysis and investment decision-making.

This app serves as a centralized platform for aggregating and analyzing financial metrics,
enabling users to track instrument performance, assess profitability, and monitor market
conditions. By integrating income, balance sheet, and ratio data with real-time market insights,
the Data app supports investors and analysts in making data-driven decisions based on historical
trends and current market dynamics.
