# Sales

The **Sales** app is a Django-based subscription management system 
that handles package purchases, payments, and user subscriptions.
It allows users to buy predefined subscription plans ([](package-model.md))
and tracks their transactions ([](payment-model.md)), linking each purchase
to an active subscription ([](subscription-model.md)). This app serves as
the backbone for monetization, providing a structured way to
manage recurring revenue models and user access tiers.

By integrating payment processing with subscription 
lifecycle management, the **Sales** app ensures accurate billing, 
access control, and renewal tracking. It enables businesses
to offer tiered plans while maintaining a clear record of user purchases 
and active subscriptions, facilitating revenue tracking and customer relationship management.
