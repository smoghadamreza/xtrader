# Social

The Social app is a Django-based platform designed to facilitate interaction
between retail traders and professional traders in a financial or cryptocurrency
trading environment. It enables users to follow verified [](pro-trader-model.md)
accounts, similar to social media follow systems, granting them visibility
into the professionals' trading activities, performance history
([](trader-history-model.md)), and potential signals. This creates a community-driven ecosystem
where less experienced traders can learn from or even automatically replicate
the trades of established professionals.

The app serves as a bridge between social networking and trading
functionality, allowing users to discover, evaluate, and connect
with [](pro-trader-model.md) based on their historical performance and risk metrics.
By incorporating follower relationships ([](follow-model.md)) and transparent
trade history tracking (([](trader-history-model.md)), the Social app enhances trust
and engagement while enabling features like copytrading or premium signal
subscriptions. This model fosters a collaborative trading environment
where expertise can be shared and monetized within the platform.
